## Reading the results

- The hybrid ranking is about twice as precise as the popularity baseline (P@10 35.7 % vs 17.3 %; random 14.0 %) and finds a relevant song in the top 5 for every user.
- Content-only is slightly ahead of the hybrid here. The ground truth is defined by the same tags the content signal uses (a persona's affinity to tags), which favours it by construction; with real users the relevance would not be tag-defined.
- Collaborative-only is close to popularity: 30 users, each hearing a different half of 80 songs, is sparse data for user-based filtering; it should improve with more users per taste.
- Personas differ: explorer (whose liked tags are common in the catalogue) benefits from every signal, melancholic gets the weakest popularity results (its taste is far from what the others like).
